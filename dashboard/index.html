How the radar prices a launch

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × chain multiplier × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier × completeness. The category multiple is the live market's own price: the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live protocols in that category, shrunk in log space toward the global median, so one outlier cannot drag a whole category. Chain breadth grows with ln(chains); maturity grows with ln(1 + age/365); a missing seven-day change costs 3%. No protocol name is ever special-cased — the same function scores every row, and the farm plan farms on the protocol's cheapest chain.

Implied value is a model, not a price, and a launch is never guaranteed. A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry.
